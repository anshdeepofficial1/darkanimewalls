Transparency

Cookies & external services.

Learn which third-party services power the public gallery and how browser data may be handled.

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Cookies on our public pages

The current public site does not deliberately set first-party marketing or advertising cookies. It does not have a visitor login system. Your browser and network may nevertheless exchange technical identifiers with external services needed for page delivery or external links.

Hosting and delivery

Vercel serves the website and serverless wallpaper API. Cloudinary supplies published artwork and images. These providers may receive technical request information (such as an IP address, requested resource, user agent and timing) as part of service delivery and security.

Fonts

The website requests typography assets from Google Fonts. When this happens, your browser connects to Google's font endpoints. See Google's privacy documentation for how it handles those requests.

Email and social links

If you choose to email us, visit Instagram, or open Outlook to compose a request, those external platforms handle their own account sessions, cookies and data under their own policies.

Your choices

Browsers typically let you clear or limit cookies, block third-party cookies and restrict tracking. Blocking certain network resources may affect font rendering, image loading, social links or email composition.

Updates

This page describes the current public site; it may change if we add new services. Contact darkanimewalls@outlook.com with questions.
