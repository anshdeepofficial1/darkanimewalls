Artwork rights

Respect the creators.

Important information about personal wallpaper downloads, fan-style designs, third-party characters and rights concerns.

Personal wallpaper use

Dark Anime Walls presents files for use as personal phone lock screens, home screens and desktop backgrounds, where permitted by applicable law and third-party rights. Downloading an image does not establish ownership of the artwork or an unrestricted license.

Commercial and redistribution rights

Do not assume that a free download permits commercial use. Selling, redistributing, printing merchandise, republishing artwork, using it in advertisements or licensing it to others may require separate written permissions from all relevant rights holders.

Fan art and recognizable characters

The gallery may include original anime-inspired designs, fan-style creative work and AI-assisted art. Names, character likenesses, brands and franchise elements belong to their respective owners. Dark Anime Walls is not endorsed by or affiliated with anime studios or franchises.

Reporting a copyright concern

Rights holders or their authorized representatives may contact darkanimewalls@outlook.com. Include:

  • The URL of the page or image you are concerned about.
  • Identification of the work or rights you believe are affected.
  • Your relationship to the rights holder and a way to contact you.
  • A clear explanation of the issue and the action requested.

We can review credible reports and consider removing or updating the relevant material. This contact route does not replace any formal legal process that may apply.

Questions about usage

If you want to republish or use a design for a project, contact us before proceeding. We can only discuss permissions for rights we actually control; third-party rights may require separate clearance.
