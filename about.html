The story

Built around the art you keep.

Dark Anime Walls is an independent wallpaper gallery focused on striking anime-inspired visuals and clean, convenient access to the published collection.

What Dark Anime Walls offers

We collect and present dark anime-inspired wallpaper designs for phone and desktop users. The catalog includes original designs, fan-style creative edits and AI-assisted artwork. Every gallery item is drawn from the actual published library, with the file resolution shown alongside the preview.

Made for real screens

Phone collections focus on portrait formats; desktop collections focus on wider compositions. The available formats and resolutions vary by image. Our goal is to make artwork easy to find without forcing visitors through an endless feed.

Independent by design

Dark Anime Walls is an independent creative project. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with anime studios, franchises or other intellectual property owners. References to characters, trademarks or fan art do not imply ownership of the underlying properties.

Community and requests

Visitors can browse available wallpapers, propose custom artwork, and discuss creative collaborations. Enquiries are voluntary and are handled separately from the public gallery.

Contact

For general questions, rights enquiries or collaboration ideas, visit the contact page or email darkanimewalls@outlook.com.
