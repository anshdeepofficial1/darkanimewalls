Frequently asked questions
Are the wallpapers free to download?

Yes. The public gallery currently offers downloads without an account or checkout. Individual rights and permitted uses are described in our Copyright & Usage page.

Can I use a wallpaper on my phone or desktop?

Yes, wallpapers are presented for personal screen-background use, subject to third-party intellectual property rights. Do not assume that a download includes permission for commercial redistribution or sale.

What do 9:16 and 16:9 mean?

9:16 is a portrait format commonly suited to phone screens. 16:9 is a landscape format commonly used for laptops and widescreen displays. Always check the actual resolution on the image card.

Can I download the original image quality?

Each card has a Download action that requests the original uploaded artwork through our download endpoint. Actual resolution and format depend on the individual file.

Why do some wallpapers have different resolutions?

The gallery contains independently uploaded artworks. Dimensions vary, and not every image is 4K. The resolution shown on each card comes from the published file.

How do I request a new anime wallpaper?

Go to the Requests page, provide a character or theme, screen format and color mood, and open the email compose window. You must send the message yourself.

Does submitting a request guarantee artwork?

No. Requests are enquiries, not automatic orders. Availability, timescales, pricing, licensing and delivery details, if applicable, are discussed separately.

Are you affiliated with any anime company?

No. Dark Anime Walls is an independent creator/gallery project. Franchise names, characters and marks are owned by their respective rights holders.

How do I report a copyright concern?

Email darkanimewalls@outlook.com with the artwork URL, the rights involved, and a contact method. See Copyright & Usage for what information helps.

Why did a wallpaper disappear?

Artworks may be withdrawn, updated, made private or scheduled by the publisher. The public API displays only currently published artwork.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can search, preview and download published wallpapers without creating a visitor account.

Is the site a native app?

No native Android or iOS app is required to access the website. You can browse directly in a modern mobile or desktop browser.
