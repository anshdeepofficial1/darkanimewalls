Privacy policy.

How the public Dark Anime Walls website operates and what data may be processed when you browse, download or contact us.

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

1. Who operates this website?

Dark Anime Walls is an independent anime wallpaper gallery. For enquiries about this policy, contact darkanimewalls@outlook.com.

2. Browsing without an account

Visitors can browse the public site without creating an account. The public gallery does not require a login and does not ask for a payment card.

3. Information processed by website services

Like most websites, hosting and delivery services may process technical information necessary to serve pages, respond to requests, deliver images, help prevent abuse and diagnose problems. This can include IP address, requested URLs, timestamps, browser/device details and network diagnostics.

The site is hosted on Vercel and images are delivered through Cloudinary. Google Fonts may be requested by your browser when rendering our interface. These providers may handle technical request data in accordance with their own privacy practices.

4. Requests and contact messages

The request and contact forms open an email compose window in your browser or email service. Submitting the form does not upload the message to a Dark Anime Walls database. You choose whether to send it. If you send a message, its contents, reply address and any other details are handled through the email provider and can be used by us to respond to your enquiry.

5. Cookies and analytics

The public site does not intentionally set its own marketing or advertising cookies. Hosting and third-party services may use cookies or other technical storage according to their operation and policies. The site does not currently include a visitor-account tracking system. See our Cookies & External Services page for more detail.

6. External links

Links to Instagram, Outlook, or other external websites open services outside our control. Their privacy policies apply once you use them.

7. Retention and deletion requests

We do not maintain a website database of public browsing accounts or submitted contact forms. For emails you actually send, you may ask us to review, correct or delete information in our correspondence, subject to legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. We cannot remove data independently retained by external providers.

8. Children

The public site is intended for general audiences interested in anime art. Do not submit sensitive personal information through an enquiry. Children should obtain appropriate guidance before contacting external services.

9. Updates and questions

We may update this policy as site features or providers change. Updated language will be shown here with a revised date. For questions or privacy requests, contact darkanimewalls@outlook.com.
